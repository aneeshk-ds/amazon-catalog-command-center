Eight questions the workbook must answer

This is the credibility spine — charts serve these, not the reverse.

1
Did the portfolio hit active targets? (rev + GP monthly)
2
Where did the money come from? (Pareto / concentration)
3
Was growth profitable? (margin vs revenue winners)
4
What dragged attainment? (inactive targets, zeros, low cohort)
5
Prime Day: plan quality vs execution?
6
Did ads pay for the deal? (BACOS vs GP%, CPU, mix)
7
Who owned what? (SEO vs PPC accountability)
8
What do we do next? (5–8 ASIN actions with € logic)

Watchouts

Parse risk · PDF text is poorly spaced — validate against €7.41M / €5.56M.
Rough sheet · Use for owner mapping only, not as a fact table.
CPU quirks · Clean #DIV/0! and PPC share > 1 before viz.
Causation · Frame as portfolio + promo/ad efficiency, not “SEO caused X.”
